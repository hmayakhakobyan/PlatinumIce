# Platinum Ice — Brand Specification

Status: Foundational document. Reflects only confirmed and approved information as of 2026-08-20. Items marked TBD or listed in "Items Still Requiring Approval" are not yet decided and must not be treated as final.

## 1. Brand Overview

- **Brand name:** Platinum Ice
- **Tagline:** Clear Luxury
- **Primary domain:** platinumice.co

Platinum Ice is a luxury clear ice brand.

## 2. Brand Positioning

Platinum Ice is a luxury clear ice brand for premium drinks, hospitality, restaurants, bars, hotels, weddings, private events, corporate events, brand activations, and direct-to-consumer ecommerce.

## 3. Brand Personality

Premium, refined, elegant, minimal luxury positioning — as stated in the approved logo direction.

## 4. Approved Logo Direction

- Crystal-clear square ice cube
- PI monogram inside the cube
- Platinum/silver metallic appearance
- PLATINUM ICE wordmark
- CLEAR LUXURY tagline
- Premium, refined, elegant, minimal luxury positioning

## 5. Logo Usage Requirements

The following logo variations will be required for production use. None of these have been designed yet — this section documents the requirement, not the deliverable.

- Primary stacked logo
- Horizontal website-header logo
- PI cube icon (standalone mark)
- Single-color logo
- Light version (for dark backgrounds)
- Dark version (for light backgrounds)
- Favicon / social icon
- Simplified engraving mark for use on actual ice

## 6. Visual Direction

High-end luxury brand feel — closer to luxury spirits, premium hospitality, jewelry, and editorial fashion brands than a delivery service or generic template.

Principles:
- Restrained luxury
- Strong typography
- Generous whitespace
- Cinematic photography/video
- A palette of premium black, platinum-silver, warm white, and subtle ice tones
- Sophisticated motion
- Minimal decoration
- Excellent mobile experience
- Product-first visual storytelling

## 7. Color Direction

Direction only — no exact values are approved.

- **Black** — premium, grounding tone
- **Platinum/silver** — metallic, refined accent
- **Warm white** — soft, elevated neutral (not stark/clinical white)
- **Subtle ice tones** — cool, translucent accents evoking clear ice

**Exact HEX / RGB values: TBD.** Do not invent or select specific color codes until approved.

## 8. Typography Direction

Direction only — no exact typefaces are approved.

- A refined luxury **display serif** for headlines/editorial moments
- A clean, modern **sans-serif** for body copy and UI text

**Exact font families: TBD.** Do not select or implement specific fonts until approved.

## 9. Photography & Video Direction

Cinematic photography/video.

Specific shot lists, styling guidelines, and production standards: **TBD.**

## 10. Motion & Animation Direction

Sophisticated motion. Avoid random animations.

Specific animation standards (timing, easing, patterns): **TBD.**

## 11. Website UI Direction

- Strong typographic hierarchy
- Generous whitespace
- Minimal decoration, no unnecessary visual effects
- Excellent mobile experience
- Product-first storytelling over dense text or marketing copy blocks

## 12. Brand Voice

Concise, sophisticated, confident, premium, restrained, and human.

Avoid generic AI marketing language and repetitive phrases such as "elevate your experience."

## 13. What Platinum Ice Must Never Look Like

- A generic ice-delivery company
- A generic WordPress template
- An AI-generated startup site
- A generic SaaS product
- A cheap ecommerce template

Also avoid, per the approved design direction: excessive gradients, glassmorphism, random animations, generic icon grids, excessive rounded cards, generic AI/SaaS layouts, unnecessary visual effects, and excessive text on luxury landing sections.
